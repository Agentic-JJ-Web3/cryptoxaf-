CryptoXAF

The product asks a stranger to send money before they receive anything. In a market where that is usually a scam, the interface is doing the persuading. So it borrows from the two documents Cameroonians already trust without thinking — the MoMo SMS receipt and the block explorer. Both are plain, machine-made and unembellished. Neither tries to sell you anything. Neither has ever needed to.

02 — Typography

One rule, learned without being told

Instrument Sans for interface and money. IBM Plex Mono for anything a machine will read back.

Why it works: Monospace reads as copy this exactly, a machine will check it. Sans reads as this is your money. Users absorb the distinction in one transaction and never mistype an address into a field that looks like a price.

04 — Status

Say where the order is, always

Manual settlement means people wait. Silence during a wait is what makes a platform feel like a scam, so every state is named in the customer's terms — never in the system's.

Waiting for your payment Checking your payment USDT sent Refund on the way
Never ship: PAYMENT_CLAIMED, PENDING_PAYOUT, TX_BROADCAST. Those are your state machine's names for things. The person waiting has no idea what they mean and reads them as evasion.
05 — Voice

Never promise what you cannot control

You settle by hand, at least at first. Copy that overstates speed converts once and then produces a furious customer, which in this market is the only marketing that actually travels.

Don'tInstant! Get your USDT in seconds 🚀

Register: sentence case everywhere, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no urgency it didn't earn. Errors state what happened and what to do next — they never apologise and they are never vague.
